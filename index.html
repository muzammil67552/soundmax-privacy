Privacy Policy

SoundMax Audio Booster • Effective Date: November 2026

Summary: SoundMax operates entirely locally inside your browser. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any audio recordings, browsing logs, or personal information.

1. Real-Time Audio Processing (No Recording)

SoundMax captures tab audio using the browser's native tabCapture API and processes it directly on your hardware via the Web Audio API (AudioContext and GainNode).

  • Audio Never Leaves Your Machine: Audio streams are amplified in memory and piped directly to your speakers. No audio is ever recorded, buffered to disk, or sent over the internet.
  • No Eavesdropping: Microphones, camera hardware, and input devices are never accessed or requested.

2. Chrome Permissions Justification

SoundMax complies strictly with Google Chrome Web Store's Least Privilege and Single Purpose policies:

  • tabCapture: Captures the active audio output stream to increase gain level beyond standard operating limits.
  • offscreen: Required by Google's Manifest V3 architecture to host the Web Audio DSP node graph without interrupting browsing.
  • tabs: Detects which tabs are actively emitting sound so you can switch media playback with a single click.
  • storage: Stores your volume slider level (e.g. 200%) and equalizer preferences locally on your machine.
  • activeTab: Grants temporary interaction with the current tab upon clicking the extension icon.

3. Payment & Licensing Data (Lemon Squeezy)

During Pro license activation, the extension connects directly to Lemon Squeezy's official licensing API and strictly to verify that your entered license key is authentic and active. We never see, collect, or store your credit card or financial details.

4. Zero Telemetry & Third-Party Trackers

SoundMax contains no Google Analytics, Facebook Pixels, tracking beacons, or device fingerprinting scripts. Your browsing history is completely private.
